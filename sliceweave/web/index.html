Demo with simulated data

Creates a case from a synthetic phantom (no patient data): three adult HASTE stacks, three adult 2-D FLAIR stacks with white-matter lesions, or six fetal stacks with slice motion.

2Load stacks

Drop DICOM folders, a .zip or NIfTI files Axial, coronal and sagittal 2-D series: single-shot T2 (HASTE / SSFSE / SSh-TSE) or 2-D FLAIR. Optional: brain masks (NIfTI named *mask*) and dcm2niix JSON sidecars.

    Files are copied to SliceWeave's temporary folder, de-identified on import, and the copies deleted.
